# Adaptive Morphology Combat — Design Foundation

**Status:** owner-confirmed vision and technical design direction, 2026-10-22. **Not** an approved final algorithm, control mapping, content grammar, build balance, or implemented system.

## Player promise
No two loot finds should need to be identical, and varied weapon/anatomy shape must *meaningfully* produce varied grips, holds, attacks and tradeoffs. This is not just random numerical stats or reskinned fixed sword/axe/staff animation banks. A new weapon's behaviour must arise from its usable physical features and the equipped fighter's current body, including body modifications. The target combat feel remains fast, readable, deflection/posture-oriented and accessible like an action game (Sekiro as tempo and readability inspiration), rather than the owner-rejected mouse-directed Mordhau/Last Oasis swings.

**Owner examples:**
- Bone lance: long form, two grips, usable forward point(s). A legitimate two-handed thrust stance and piercing attacks derived from grips/reach/piercing features, potentially hooks if actual geometry supports them.
- Axe: handles and a side-mounted cutting head; favor swings/chops, not a generic dagger-style stab merely because the item is labeled axe.
- Bo staff: two spaced grips and two useful striking ends; naturally alternate leading/rear striking ends, with resulting hand motions and transitions.
- Modified bodies: available limbs, joints, range, grasp topology, strength and stance constraints change which grips and moves can be performed. Oversized or multiple-limb anatomy may enable novel forms, but must remain readable and performant.

## Crucial architecture: generation -> perception -> action
1. **Constrained shape generator:** generate seed-defined connected weapon structure (shaft/haft/blade/tips/guards/etc.) and body morphology from grammar + budgets. Tag/calculate grip volumes, local axes, safe hand clearances, attack surfaces and their normals/tangents, mass distribution/center of mass/inertia, reach, collision/swept shapes, dangerous edges/points, flexibility, and attachment requirements. Gameplay features must correspond to geometry, not arbitrary names. Visual mesher consumes the **same generated geometry descriptor** (not an independent design that disagrees with collision or grip data).
2. **Anatomy/equipment legality solver:** evaluate limbs, hands/claws, reach, joint angles, joint limits, feasible hand-to-grip placement, 1H/2H/alternative holds, clearance, shield/augmentation conflicts, posture, balance and locomotion. Return valid stable grips/stances or reject/regenerate a physically unusable item. Generated body plans may need new compatible locomotion/animation families; do not assume unlimited generic humanoid IK solves everything.
3. **Combat-affordance inference:** derive eligible verbs from *actual surfaces and valid grasp*: thrust if pointed forward and controllable, chop/cut if usable edge/face and swing clearance, blunt strike for staff/mace, hook/pull only with real hook geometry, guard/parry with suitable contact surface/strength, alternating ends with double-ended implements. Score feasible actions on reach, tempo, energy, exposed posture, damage channels and distinct silhouette. Geometry + anatomy produces a capability set; item labels may assist UI only, never grant imaginary actions. Don't hardcode 'axe' = fixed animation.
4. **Motion synthesis/retargeting:** finite attack **intent vocabulary**, deliberately authored quality motion archetypes/poses or motion primitives and parametrized phase curves combined with IK/two-hand constraints, joint-limits, footwork/root motion and contact paths. Derive per-item light/heavy combos, stance/grip shifts and defensive motion from selected feasible actions, with shared timebase controlling windup/active/recovery/posture/stamina and actual swept contact. Avoid wholly free-form procedural limb twitching or independent 'physics-only' combat: add robust authored style and procedural adaptation. Accept variation without making animations incoherent.
5. **Combat legibility and balance:** the same affordance resolver powers player, enemies and asynchronous Echo snapshots; attacks must telegraph via distinctive trajectories/poses/tempo; fair deflectable impact timing, clear types of damage and deterministic reproducibility from seed plus versioned generator/content rules; server later checks generated definition and legal move lists. Prevent unreachable, uncounterable or wildly imbalanced random items with budgets, constraints and validation. Unlimited uniqueness is not promised: finite primitives, combinations, and constraints enable enormous variety without every seed being structurally unique.
6. **Presentation:** visual generator uses the same topology, collision anchors and socket conventions; style layer provides artistic shape/material/ornament variation without changing the combat contract. LOD, rig sockets, physics surfaces, readable silhouettes and accessible cues are validated.
7. **Content progression:** salvage whole generated equipment or body parts; versioned provenance and frozen rolled instance/seed matter for offline replay and ranked Echo legitimacy. Distinguish definition generation from instance rolls, equipped state, and authored or gained abilities. Body mods must affect mechanics and animation; don't turn all mods into Q-button attacks.

## Proposed proof before production overhaul
An isolated **Adaptive Combat Lab** on Godot 4.7.2 with one current rig + opponent and about 3 procedural families **inferred through features** rather than item labels:
- one long thrusting bone lance with at least two seed-grip variations;
- one off-axis cutting axe with shifting weight;
- one double-ended staff with wide grips and alternating ends;
- one limb/body modification that demonstrably changes a permitted grip/attack.
Randomize 10-20 variations with visibly and mechanically distinct derived legal light/heavy/guard actions. Include negative cases (unreachable grips, shaft through torso, implausible dual-grip reach, no valid striking surface, too much inertia for anatomy). Maintain understandable telegraphs and Sekiro-like defend/dodge/posture, fixed light/heavy input *intent* with physical/animation adaptation. Compare feel with old labs, ask for owner playtest before integration. Existing directional expedition/showcase stay untouched.
- Test determinism, input-to-impact windows, IK/joint limits, grip stability, weapon-vs-swept-contact geometry, opposing deflection/posture legality, player/AI parity, weapon family variation, no collision tunneling, no unintended generated attack types, item-instance serialization and limited performance budget.
- Architecture gate: **data contracts and shape/affordance prototype first**, then 3D visual proof and responsive combat; do not task an agent to build an unlimited procgen system in one PR. Two bounded PRs could be: Lab independent research/validator + Game isolated Godot test bed, with shared agreed interface and no conflicting source edits.
- Reuse existing `docs/EQUIPMENT_FOUNDATION.md`, `docs/ANIMATION_PIPELINE.md`, anatomy graph, visual showcase 22-bone rig/sockets, combat resource/status, Lab content proposal, and Godot regression harness. Reassess their assumptions (especially fixed humanoid topology, single weapon contact segment, authored bone animations, and hardcoded grip/weapon classes), rather than preserve obsolete restrictions by default.

**Decision still needed by actual feel tests:** how many authored gesture primitives are needed to make procedural adaptation natural and weighty, and which body silhouettes need separate movement families. Accept that fully arbitrary anatomy/weapon meshes with universally good generated animations is not a credible v1 promise.

## Clarification: no million-item description catalog required — 2026-10-22

The owner asks whether an arbitrary generated shovel would intrinsically contain enough machine-readable functional detail, rather than relying on ChatGPT knowing what a shovel usually does. **It must.** Combat may never infer a move merely from an item's human-readable label, a text description, image recognition or a free-form LLM guess. This section makes the required generation contract explicit.

- Build a modest **parametric grammar of structural primitives** (shafts, branching frames, blades, striking surfaces, tips, hooks, guards, sockets), **material distributions** (density, stiffness, toughness, edge durability and appropriate bounded fantasy modifiers), **assembly rules**, grip regions and body morphology. Generate an object graph and exact reusable mesh/collision geometry from the *same canonical seed/recipe*. Functional semantics are linked to generated parts and local frame features.
- Derive measurable geometry: valid continuous grasp intervals; diameter and hand clearance; centers of mass and tensor/inertia estimates; swept collision shapes; reach; point geometry/tangent/normal; edge sharpness and support; blunt area; connectivity/rigidity; hand occupancy; stress/load constraints. **Authoring tags describe *material/part physics*, not final move lists.** A sharpened leading tip could enable thrust if reachable and aligned; a shovel-like slab without such a tip should not receive that attack merely because of its name. Named archetypes/templates can bias *generation* for coherent artifacts, but must not substitute for capability validation.
- Feature extraction + constrained **affordance solver** output feasible (body, object, grip, stance, motion) tuples. Animation synthesizer maps accepted tuples to quality choreographed primitives + IK. Use stable intent controls and stateful timing; reject unsafe, unreachable, clipping, or incoherent configurations. Appearance styling/rarity/name generator may draw on structure, materials, provenance and granted supernatural traits only **after** underlying item definition exists. Explicit fantasy traits can add physical capabilities but must provide real representation and combat rules, not merely flavor text.
- At scale store **versioned generation recipe + seed + independently verifiable resolved descriptor** (and eventual server-issued earned-ownership proof), not a hand-authored document for every potential variant. Procedural meshes and descriptions are reproducible derived outputs. A large *live* player economy may eventually store concrete owned item-instance records, but that differs from authoring millions of prototypes by hand.
- Separate layers: (1) structural recipe and measured affordances (realized geometry), (2) balance/quality/rarity and explicit magic traits, (3) renderer/LOD/material visuals, (4) player-facing procedural name/lore text, (5) permitted combat action graph. Don't imply these are interchangeable, and don't let text or cosmetics grant unsupported attacks.
- Initial proof should make **unnamed** randomized held objects behave appropriately, with developers inspecting derived features and counterexample rejections before any 'legendary shovel' name is assigned. Art-authored or handcrafted items may still exist and use exactly the same descriptor/API.

**Important engineering caveat:** automatic recovery of semantics from a completely arbitrary polygon mesh is *much harder* and may require expensive geometry analysis or manual annotations. Start with procedurally generated structured meshes that intrinsically carry the metadata. Later importing arbitrary player-created meshes is a separate research problem.

## Object-first improvised equipment — clarified 2026-10-22

The generation space explicitly includes **general objects**, not just weapons or weapon components. A folded umbrella, shovel, broken furniture, botanical object, ritual implement or unfamiliar assembled artifact may be usable in combat when its generated physical structure and the wearer's body allow it. Some objects should be ineffective or unusable.

Use a broad assembly vocabulary (supports, surfaces, branches, frames, ribs, flexible joints and material types) to create structured objects. Their generated descriptors include graspable regions, surfaces, motion constraints, geometry and physical properties. Appearance and functional metadata derive from the same underlying representation. Names and aesthetic descriptions do not authorize attacks.

The object-and-anatomy affordance solver produces possible holding arrangements and actions. Adaptive animations map intentional player inputs onto appropriate motions and guard poses. Test with seeded anonymous objects representing an umbrella, shovel, pole, unusual household object and deliberately awkward noncombat shape. Verify changing labels does not change combat, while changing structure does. Include validation failures and visibly distinct results. Initial Godot work should be an isolated playable prototype with no changes to existing browser gameplay.
